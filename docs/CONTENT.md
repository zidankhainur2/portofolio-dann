## 1. Content Principles

The portfolio copy should be:

- concise,
- engineering-oriented,
- confident without exaggeration,
- easy to scan,
- specific about technologies and outcomes,
- consistent with a fresh-graduate profile that already has practical experience.

### Tone

Use:

- professional,
- precise,
- curious,
- modern,
- approachable.

Avoid:

- inflated claims,
- “guru”, “expert”, or “master” language without evidence,
- generic phrases such as “passionate developer” as the main differentiator,
- excessive buzzwords,
- long paragraphs inside visual sections.

## 11. Content Density Rules

### Hero

Maximum:

- 1 identity heading,
- 1 supporting paragraph,
- 2 primary actions,
- small social shortcuts.

### About

Maximum:

- 2 short paragraphs,
- 3 stack groups.

### Experience

Each role:

- company,
- role,
- period,
- 3–5 stack labels,
- one short paragraph.

### Projects

Each card:

- title,
- category,
- status,
- 1 short description,
- 3–5 important technologies,
- 1–2 actions.

### Research

Each publication:

- title,
- metadata,
- 2-sentence contribution summary,
- one authoritative action.

### Certifications

Do not show certificate descriptions for every Dicoding card. Keep descriptions only for highlighted certifications.

---

## 12. Public vs Private Project Copy Rules

### Public

Allowed:

- repository link,
- live demo,
- implementation details,
- architecture notes.

### Private

Use wording such as:

- `Private Build`
- `Case Study Available`
- `UI Gallery`
- `Architecture Overview`

Do not imply the repository is publicly accessible.

---

## 13. English / Indonesian Language Strategy

The recommended V1 approach is:

- English for compact section titles and technical labels.
- Indonesian for explanatory copy.
- Technology names remain in their standard English form.

Example:

> **DEEP DIVE INTO MY WORKS**  
> Eksplorasi proyek aplikasi web, integrasi AI, hingga utilitas produktivitas yang telah saya bangun.

This preserves personality without sacrificing clarity for Indonesian recruiters.

A future bilingual mode may be implemented if needed, but is not required for V1.
